# Valentine's Day Consumer Spending Analysis

This project analyzes historical Valentine's Day spending by retail category.

The visualization shows spending trends over time to help inform inventory planning.

Run `analysis.R` to reproduce the analysis.

Based on the data, we should give jewelry the largest share of our purchasing budget and increase spending on clothing and gift cards. Since all products are equally profitable, customer spending trends can help us choose which categories to support.

Jewelry had the highest spending among physical goods in 2022, at $45.75 per person. This increased by about 49% from 2021. However, jewelry spending changed from year to year, so we should avoid expecting another large increase.
Clothing and gift cards also show growth. Clothing spending increased from $10.93 in 2010 to $21.46 in 2022. Gift card spending rose from $8.42 to $17.22 during the same period. Both categories continued growing between 2021 and 2022.

We should keep flowers and candy available because spending on both increased in 2022. Order flowers in smaller batches to reduce waste. Give greeting cards a smaller budget because spending fell from $8.48 in 2021 to $7.47 in 2022. Evening-out spending is high, but it does not represent physical stock.

Total spending per person increased by 6.5% in 2022, but remained below the 2020 peak. Also, fewer people celebrated in 2022 than in 2010. These results do not support increasing all inventory equally.

Finally, the data ends in 2022 and shows dollars spent, not units bought. We should check our recent sales and product prices before deciding how many items to order.
